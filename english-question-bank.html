A. Subject–verb agreement (Q1–Q8)

Q1. Each of the students ___ a book. a) have b) has c) are having d) were

Answer: b) has — "Each" is singular. (Rule 1)

Q2. Neither the manager nor the employees ___ present. a) was b) were c) is d) be

Answer: b) were — The verb agrees with the nearer subject, "employees". (Rule 3)

Q3. The number of applicants ___ increasing every year. a) are b) is c) were d) have

Answer: b) is — "The number of" takes a singular verb. (Rule 2)

Q4. A number of students ___ absent today. a) is b) was c) are d) has

Answer: c) are — "A number of" takes a plural verb. (Rule 2)

Q5. The committee ___ taken its decision. a) have b) has c) are d) were

Answer: b) has — A collective noun acting as one unit is singular. (Rule 4)

Q6. Every one of the boys ___ punished. a) were b) was c) are d) have

Answer: b) was — "Every one" is singular. (Rule 1)

Q7. Mathematics ___ my favourite subject. a) are b) is c) were d) have

Answer: b) is — Subjects ending in -s (mathematics, physics, economics, news) are singular. (Rule 5)

Q8. Neither of the answers ___ correct. a) are b) is c) were d) have

Answer: b) is — "Neither of" is singular. (Rule 1)

B. Prepositions (Q9–Q20)

Q9. He has been living here ___ 2015. a) for b) since c) from d) in

Answer: b) since — "Since" is used with a point of time. (Rule 6)

Q10. I have known him ___ ten years. a) since b) for c) from d) at

Answer: b) for — "For" is used with a period of time. (Rule 6)

Q11. She is senior ___ me. a) than b) to c) of d) from

Answer: b) to — Senior, junior, superior, inferior take "to". (Rule 7)

Q12. He is married ___ her. a) with b) to c) for d) of

Answer: b) to — Married to. (Rule 7)

Q13. The teacher was angry ___ the students. a) at b) with c) on d) of

Answer: b) with — Angry with a person; angry at a thing. (Rule 7)

Q14. He insisted ___ going alone. a) to b) on c) for d) of

Answer: b) on — Insist on. (Rule 7)

Q15. She is good ___ mathematics. a) in b) at c) on d) for

Answer: b) at — Good at a subject/skill. (Rule 7)

Q16. He died ___ cholera. a) from b) of c) by d) with

Answer: b) of — Die of a disease. (Rule 7)

Q17. The book consists ___ five chapters. a) in b) of c) on d) with

Answer: b) of — Consist of. (Rule 8)

Q18. He is accused ___ theft. a) for b) of c) with d) on

Answer: b) of — Accused of. (Rule 7)

Q19. I prefer tea ___ coffee. a) than b) to c) over d) from

Answer: b) to — Prefer X to Y. (Rule 8)

Q20. He congratulated me ___ my success. a) for b) on c) about d) with

Answer: b) on — Congratulate on. (Rule 7)

C. Conditionals, subjunctive & special structures (Q21–Q27)

Q21. If I ___ you, I would apologise. a) am b) was c) were d) be

Answer: c) were — Second conditional uses "were" for all persons. (Rule 9)

Q22. If he had studied, he ___ passed. a) would b) would have c) had d) will have

Answer: b) would have — Third conditional. (Rule 10)

Q23. Had I known the truth, I ___ come. a) would b) would have c) will d) had

Answer: b) would have — Inverted third conditional. (Rule 11)

Q24. He speaks as if he ___ everything. a) knows b) knew c) know d) had known

Answer: b) knew — "As if" takes the past subjunctive for an unreal present. (Rule 12)

Q25. It is high time we ___ the meeting. a) start b) starts c) started d) will start

Answer: c) started — "It is high time" + past simple. (Rule 13)

Q26. I would rather ___ than beg. a) to die b) die c) dying d) died

Answer: b) die — "Would rather" + base verb. (Rule 14)

Q27. He cannot help ___ at the joke. a) to laugh b) laugh c) laughing d) laughed

Answer: c) laughing — "Cannot help" + gerund. (Rule 15)

D. Gerund vs infinitive (Q28–Q33)

Q28. Would you mind ___ the window? a) to open b) open c) opening d) opened

Answer: c) opening — "Mind" + gerund. (Rule 15)

Q29. She is used to ___ up early. a) get b) getting c) to get d) got

Answer: b) getting — "Be used to" + gerund. (Rule 15)

Q30. He refused ___ the offer. a) accepting b) to accept c) accept d) accepted

Answer: b) to accept — "Refuse" + infinitive. (Rule 16)

Q31. They decided ___ the project. a) postponing b) to postpone c) postpone d) postponed

Answer: b) to postpone — "Decide" + infinitive. (Rule 16)

Q32. I look forward to ___ from you. a) hear b) hearing c) to hear d) heard

Answer: b) hearing — "Look forward to" + gerund. (Rule 15)

Q33. He avoided ___ the question. a) to answer b) answering c) answer d) answered

Answer: b) answering — "Avoid" + gerund. (Rule 15)

E. Relative pronouns, voice & narration (Q34–Q41)

Q34. The man ___ car was stolen reported it to the police. a) who b) whom c) whose d) which

Answer: c) whose — Possession. (Rule 17)

Q35. This is the boy ___ won the prize. a) whom b) whose c) who d) which

Answer: c) who — Subject of the clause. (Rule 17)

Q36. The book ___ I bought yesterday is new. a) who b) whose c) which d) whom

Answer: c) which — Thing. (Rule 17)

Q37. Choose the correct passive form of "They built the bridge." a) The bridge is built. b) The bridge was built. c) The bridge has built. d) The bridge were built.

Answer: b) The bridge was built. (Rule 21)

Q38. Choose the correct passive form of "Who broke the glass?" a) Who was the glass broken? b) By whom was the glass broken? c) Whom broke the glass? d) The glass broke by who?

Answer: b) By whom was the glass broken? (Rule 21)

Q39. He said, "I am ill." → Indirect speech: a) He said that he is ill. b) He said that he was ill. c) He says that he was ill. d) He said that I was ill.

Answer: b) He said that he was ill. (Rule 22)

Q40. He asked, "Where do you live?" → Indirect speech: a) He asked where do I live. b) He asked where I lived. c) He asked where did I live. d) He asked where I live.

Answer: b) He asked where I lived. (Rule 22)

Q41. He said, "Do you know me?" → Indirect speech: a) He asked if I knew him. b) He asked if I know him. c) He asked that I knew him. d) He said if I knew him.

Answer: a) He asked if I knew him. (Rule 22)

F. Spelling (Q42–Q46)

Q42. Choose the correctly spelled word. a) accomodation b) accommodation c) acommodation d) accommodetion

Answer: b) accommodation — double c, double m.

Q43. Choose the correctly spelled word. a) occurence b) ocurrence c) occurrence d) occurrance

Answer: c) occurrence — double c, double r.

Q44. Choose the correctly spelled word. a) maintainance b) maintenance c) maintenence d) maintanence

Answer: b) maintenance

Q45. Choose the correctly spelled word. a) commitee b) committe c) committee d) comittee

Answer: c) committee — double m, double t, double e.

Q46. Choose the correctly spelled word. a) embarass b) embarras c) embarrass d) embaras

Answer: c) embarrass — double r, double s.

G. Vocabulary (Q47–Q52)

Q47. Synonym of abundant: a) scarce b) plentiful c) rare d) limited

Answer: b) plentiful

Q48. Antonym of transparent: a) clear b) obvious c) opaque d) visible

Answer: c) opaque

Q49. Synonym of candid: a) frank b) secretive c) rude d) shy

Answer: a) frank

Q50. Antonym of frugal: a) thrifty b) economical c) extravagant d) careful

Answer: c) extravagant

Q51. Synonym of meticulous: a) careless b) very careful c) lazy d) quick

Answer: b) very careful

Q52. Antonym of obsolete: a) outdated b) ancient c) modern d) old

Answer: c) modern

H. Idioms & analogies (Q53–Q58)

Q53. "To bite the bullet" means: a) to eat quickly b) to endure pain bravely c) to make a mistake d) to speak rudely

Answer: b) to endure pain bravely

Q54. "A blessing in disguise" means: a) a hidden gift b) an apparent misfortune that turns out well c) a religious ceremony d) a costly mistake

Answer: b) an apparent misfortune that turns out well

Q55. "To beat about the bush" means: a) to search in a forest b) to avoid the main point c) to work hard d) to speak loudly

Answer: b) to avoid the main point

Q56. "In lieu of" means: a) in addition to b) instead of c) because of d) in spite of

Answer: b) instead of

Q57. Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ? a) Student b) School c) Book d) Lesson

Answer: b) School — workplace of the professional.

Q58. Pen : Write :: Knife : ? a) Sharp b) Cut c) Metal d) Kitchen

Answer: b) Cut — tool : function.

I. Articles & uncountables (Q59–Q60)

Q59. She is ___ honest girl. a) a b) an c) the d) no article

Answer: b) an — "Honest" begins with a vowel sound. (Rule 20)

Q60. The scenery of Sylhet ___ beautiful. a) are b) is c) were d) have

Answer: b) is — "Scenery" is uncountable and singular. (Rule 5)
