2. The 10 formulas

  1. Profit% = (SP − CP)/CP × 100
  2. Successive change: a + b + ab/100
  3. Discount equivalent: a + b − ab/100
  4. CP of x = SP of y → profit% = (x − y)/y × 100
  5. Work together = ab/(a + b)
  6. Man-days: M₁D₁ = M₂D₂
  7. Average speed (equal distance) = 2xy/(x + y)
  8. Train over platform: (L₁ + L₂)/speed
  9. CI − SI (2 yrs) = P(r/100)²
  10. n(A∪B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A∩B)

Conversions: km/h → m/s × 5/18 · m/s → km/h × 18/5 · π = 22/7 when r is a multiple of 7.

Squares: 11²=121, 12²=144, 13²=169, 14²=196, 15²=225, 16²=256, 17²=289, 18²=324, 19²=361, 25²=625.

3. The 8 grammar rules that appear every time

  1. Each / every / either / neither / everyone → singular verb
  2. The number of → singular; a number of → plural
  3. Neither…nor / either…or → verb agrees with the nearer subject
  4. Since + point of time; for + period
  5. Senior / junior / superior / inferior / married / addicted → to
  6. Good at · accused of · insist on · consist of · die of · prefer X to Y
  7. If + past, would + base (2nd); If + had + V3, would have + V3 (3rd)
  8. Mind / enjoy / avoid / look forward to / used to / cannot help → -ing

Spelling five: accommodation · occurrence · maintenance · committee · embarrass.

Ctrl+C copy · Ctrl+V paste · Ctrl+X cut · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo · Ctrl+S save · Ctrl+P print · Ctrl+F find · Ctrl+A select all · F5 slideshow · F12 Save As · Alt+F4 close.

5. Exam-hall timings

Minutes Action
0–2 Fill every box; check roll number twice
2–40 First pass: everything you can do in 40 seconds
40–55 Second pass: medium questions
55–65 Third pass: educated guesses; no blanks
Written Math first → paragraph → essay
Last 5 Check that the bubbling matches the question numbers

If you are stuck: skip, mark, return. One question is never worth three.

6. Bag list (pack tonight)

  • Admit card (printed, 2 copies)
  • Original NID
  • 2 black ballpoint pens (+1 spare)
  • Pencil and eraser (for rough work, if allowed)
  • Watch (non-digital, if permitted)
  • Water bottle (clear)
  • Directions to the centre checked the night before

Not allowed: mobile phone, calculator, smart/digital watch, any electronic device.

7. The night before

  • No new topics. No mock tests after 6 PM.
  • Skim this sheet once, then close everything.
  • Lay out clothes, admit card and pens by the door.
  • Sleep by 10:30 PM. Sleep beats one more hour of revision — every time.

8. The morning of the exam

  • Light breakfast; avoid heavy or oily food.
  • Reach the centre 60–75 minutes early.
  • Do not discuss answers or "suggestions" with the crowd outside. It only creates doubt.
  • Read page 1 instructions: check for negative marking before you guess blindly.

9. Three sentences to remember for the written part

  1. Opening: "…is not merely a matter of X; it is a question of Y."
  2. Middle: "This matters for Bangladesh because…"
  3. Closing: "The task, therefore, is not to celebrate X but to ensure that Y."

Structure earns marks. Length does not.
