1. Percentage

  • x% of N = (x/100) × N
  • What % is A of B? = (A/B) × 100
  • % increase = (Increase ÷ Original) × 100
  • % decrease = (Decrease ÷ Original) × 100
  • Successive change a% then b%: net = a + b + (ab/100)
  • +20% then −20% → 20 − 20 − 400/100 = −4%
  • −10% then −20% → −10 − 20 + 2 = −28% (i.e. 28% discount)
  • Price rises by r% → consumption must fall by [r/(100+r)] × 100
  • Price +25% → cut 20%
  • Price falls by r% → consumption can rise by [r/(100−r)] × 100

2. Profit & Loss

  • Profit = SP − CP; Loss = CP − SP
  • Profit% = (Profit/CP) × 100 (always on cost price unless stated)
  • SP = CP × (100 + P%)/100
  • CP = SP × 100/(100 + P%)
  • Discount% = (MP − SP)/MP × 100 (always on marked price)
  • MP = SP × 100/(100 − D%)
  • Successive discounts a%, b% → single equivalent = a + b − ab/100
  • CP of x articles = SP of y articles → Profit% = [(x − y)/y] × 100
  • CP of 20 = SP of 16 → profit = (20−16)/16 × 100 = 25%

3. Ratio & Proportion

  • a : b = ka : kb. To combine A:B and B:C, make the B terms equal.
  • Divide N in ratio a:b:c → parts = N × a/(a+b+c), etc.
  • Partnership: profit ratio = (capital × time) ratio

4. Average

  • Average = Sum ÷ Count
  • Sum = Average × Count
  • New average after adding = (old sum + new value) ÷ (n + 1)
  • Average of 1 to n = (n + 1)/2
  • Average of consecutive numbers = middle term
  • Average speed (equal distances) = 2xy/(x + y) — not (x+y)/2

5. Time & Work

  • If A finishes in a days, 1 day's work = 1/a
  • A and B together: 1/a + 1/b → time = ab/(a+b)
  • Three workers: 1/a + 1/b + 1/c
  • Work done = rate × time; remaining = 1 − work done
  • Man-days: M₁D₁ = M₂D₂ (same work)
  • Pipes: filling is +, emptying is −

6. Speed, Distance, Time

  • Speed = Distance/Time; Distance = Speed × Time
  • km/h → m/s: × 5/18. m/s → km/h: × 18/5
  • Train crossing a pole: time = length of train ÷ speed
  • Train crossing a bridge/platform: time = (train + bridge) ÷ speed
  • Opposite directions: relative speed = sum
  • Same direction: relative speed = difference
  • Boat: downstream = b + s, upstream = b − s
  • b = (down + up)/2, s = (down − up)/2

7. Interest

  • Simple Interest = P × r × t / 100
  • Amount (SI) = P + SI = P(1 + rt/100)
  • Compound Amount = P(1 + r/100)ⁿ
  • CI = P[(1 + r/100)ⁿ − 1]
  • Difference between CI and SI for 2 years = P(r/100)²
  • Difference for 3 years = P(r/100)²(3 + r/100)

8. Mixture & Alligation

  • Component amount = Total × fraction
  • Adding pure substance x to make new concentration: (original component + x) / (total + x) = target fraction
  • Alligation rule: ratio of cheap : dear = (dear price − mean) : (mean − cheap price)

9. Sets (Venn)

  • n(A∪B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A∩B)
  • n(A∪B∪C) = n(A)+n(B)+n(C) − n(A∩B) − n(B∩C) − n(C∩A) + n(A∩B∩C)
  • Exactly two = [sum of pairwise intersections] − 3 × (all three)
  • Only A = n(A) − n(A∩B) − n(A∩C) + n(A∩B∩C)
  • Total = n(A∪B∪C) + none

10. Geometry & Mensuration

Shape Area Perimeter
Rectangle l × b 2(l + b)
Square a² 4a
Triangle ½ × b × h sum of sides
Circle πr² 2πr
Parallelogram b × h 2(a + b)
Trapezium ½(a + b)h —
  • Square diagonal = a√2 → if diagonal d, area = d²/2
  • Circle: diameter = 2r; circumference = πd
  • Use π = 22/7 when the radius is a multiple of 7; otherwise 3.14

11. Series patterns

  • Difference constant → arithmetic
  • Ratio constant → geometric
  • Differences 4, 6, 8, 10 → next +12 (pattern n(n+1))
  • ×2 + 1 → 3, 7, 15, 31, 63
  • Squares: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36
  • Cubes: 1, 8, 27, 64, 125

12. Probability & counting

  • Probability = favourable ÷ total
  • Two dice: total outcomes = 36
  • One card: total = 52 (4 suits × 13)
  • nPr = n!/(n−r)! ; nCr = n!/[r!(n−r)!]
  • Arrangements of a word = n! / (repeats!)
