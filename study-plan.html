02 · 12-Day Study Plan (8 Nov → 20 Nov 2026)

Practice

Rule of the plan: 3 hours of active work per day beats 8 hours of reading. Active = you write the answer before looking at the solution.

Daily shape (adjust to your schedule):

Block Time What
A 60 min New topic — read the rule/formula, then do 10 questions
B 45 min Mixed drill from a different subject (spaced repetition)
C 30 min Written practice — one paragraph or one math with full steps
D 15 min Review mistakes in mistake-tracker.csv

Week 1 — Build the base

Day 1 — Sun 8 Nov · Math core

  • Read 03-Math/Math-Formula-Sheet.md fully.
  • Do Q1–Q15 of Math-Question-Bank.md (percentage, profit-loss, discount).
  • Write the 6 formulas from memory at the end. If you cannot, re-read.

Day 2 — Mon 9 Nov · Math core II

  • Q16–Q30 (ratio, average, partnership, age).
  • 20 minutes: English grammar rules 1–8.
  • Write one 120-word paragraph: "Why accuracy matters in a bank branch."

Day 3 — Tue 10 Nov · Math core III

  • Q31–Q45 (time & work, pipes, speed, train, boat).
  • 15 ICT questions.
  • Re-do every question you got wrong on Day 1–2.

Day 4 — Wed 11 Nov · Math finish + English

  • Q46–Q60 (interest, mixture, sets, geometry, series, probability).
  • English rules 9–16 + Q1–Q20 of English bank.
  • Write one formal email (see 09-Written/Model-Answers.md, Model 5).

Day 5 — Thu 12 Nov · English deep

  • English rules 17–25.
  • English Q21–Q45.
  • Learn 30 idioms from the idiom list. Test yourself by covering the meaning column.

Day 6 — Fri 13 Nov · Bangla + GK

  • Bangla Q1–Q40.
  • Read 06-General-Knowledge/Banking-GK-Notes.md once, slowly.
  • Memorise the 12 numbers in the "Numbers you must know" table.

Day 7 — Sat 14 Nov · GK + ICT consolidation

  • GK Q1–Q30.
  • ICT Q1–Q30.
  • Mock Test 2 (50 Q, 45 min, timed). Mark it. Log every mistake.

Week 2 — Convert knowledge into marks

Day 8 — Sun 15 Nov · Analytical

  • 08-Analytical/Analytical-Question-Bank.md Q1–Q30.
  • Redo the logic games you got wrong; these are learnable patterns.
  • 20 GK questions from the banking section.

Day 9 — Mon 16 Nov · Written part

  • Read 09-Written/Written-Part-Guide.md.
  • Write 3 answers by hand, timed: one paragraph (10 min), one argument (15 min), one math with steps (10 min).
  • Compare against Model-Answers.md. Fix structure, not vocabulary.

Day 10 — Tue 17 Nov · Full mock

  • Mock Test 1 (100 Q, 90 min, timed, no phone).
  • Mark it. Compute section-wise accuracy.
  • Spend the rest of the session on your two weakest sections only.

Day 11 — Wed 18 Nov · Repair day

  • Re-do every wrong question from both mocks and the whole week.
  • Memorise 11-Revision/Last-48-Hours.md.
  • One essay + one paragraph, handwritten, timed.

Day 12 — Thu 19 Nov · Taper

  • Morning: skim formula sheet, grammar rules, GK numbers.
  • Afternoon: 30 easy questions to build confidence. No new topics.
  • Evening: pack admit card, 2 black pens, NID. Sleep by 10:30 PM.

Exam day — Fri 20 Nov

  • Light breakfast. Reach the centre 60–75 minutes early.
  • Do not discuss answers with anyone before entering.
  • Follow the exam-hall plan in 01-Exam-Brief.md.

If you only have 3 days

Day Do this
Day 1 Math formula sheet + Math Q1–Q45 + English rules 1–25
Day 2 Mock Test 1 + review every mistake + Banking GK numbers
Day 3 Mock Test 2 + Last-48-Hours.md + one handwritten essay and one paragraph

If you only have 1 day

  1. 11-Revision/Last-48-Hours.md (60 min)
  2. Math formula sheet + Q1–Q30 (90 min)
  3. English rules + Q1–Q30 (60 min)
  4. Banking GK numbers + ICT shortcuts (45 min)
  5. One timed 50-question mock, then review (60 min)
