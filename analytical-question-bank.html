Analytical questions look scary and are actually pattern machines. Learn the five patterns below and you can solve most of them in under a minute.

The five patterns: series → find the rule; coding → find the shift; blood relation → draw a tree; direction → draw axes; seating → draw slots.

A. Series (Q1–Q4)

Q1. 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ___ a) 35 b) 36 c) 37 d) 38

Answer: c) 37 Differences: 3, 5, 7, 9 → next 11 → 26 + 11 = 37.

Q2. 3, 8, 15, 24, 35, ___ a) 44 b) 46 c) 48 d) 50

Answer: c) 48 Differences: 5, 7, 9, 11 → next 13 → 35 + 13 = 48. (These are n² − 1: 2²−1, 3²−1, 4²−1 … 7²−1 = 48.)

Q3. AZ, BY, CX, DW, ___ a) EV b) EU c) FV d) DV

Answer: a) EV First letters go forward A, B, C, D, E. Second letters go backward Z, Y, X, W, V.

Q4. 7, 14, 28, 56, ___ a) 84 b) 96 c) 112 d) 120

Answer: c) 112 Each term doubles: 56 × 2 = 112.

B. Coding–decoding (Q5–Q8)

Q5. If CAT is coded as 3-1-20, how is DOG coded? a) 4-14-7 b) 4-15-7 c) 3-15-7 d) 4-15-6

Answer: b) 4-15-7 Letters are replaced by their alphabet positions: D=4, O=15, G=7.

Q6. In a certain code, BANK is written as CBOL. How is LOAN written? a) MPBO b) MPBN c) NPBO d) KPBO

Answer: a) MPBO Each letter moves one step forward: L→M, O→P, A→B, N→O.

Q7. If A = 1, B = 2, C = 3 … what is the value of the word CAB? a) 5 b) 6 c) 7 d) 8

Answer: b) 6 C=3, A=1, B=2 → 3 + 1 + 2 = 6.

Q8. If FRIEND is coded as HTKGPF, how is CANDLE coded? a) ECPFNG b) ECPGNF c) DBPFNG d) ECPFMG

Answer: a) ECPFNG Each letter moves two steps forward: C→E, A→C, N→P, D→F, L→N, E→G.

C. Blood relations (Q9–Q12)

Q9. A is B's brother. C is B's mother. D is C's father. How is A related to D? a) Son b) Grandson c) Grandfather d) Nephew

Answer: b) Grandson C is A's mother; D is C's father → D is A's maternal grandfather → A is D's grandson.

Q10. Pointing to a photograph, a man said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is she related to him? a) Mother b) Aunt c) Sister d) Cousin

Answer: c) Sister Grandfather's only son = the man's father. His father's daughter = his sister.

Q11. X is the mother of Y. Y is the husband of Z. How is X related to Z? a) Sister b) Mother-in-law c) Aunt d) Daughter-in-law

Answer: b) Mother-in-law

Q12. If "A + B" means A is the father of B, and "A − B" means A is the sister of B, then in "M + N − O", how is M related to O? a) Brother b) Father c) Uncle d) Son

Answer: b) Father M is the father of N; N is the sister of O → M is also the father of O.

D. Direction sense (Q13–Q16)

Q13. A man walks 5 km north, turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right and walks 5 km. How far is he from the starting point? a) 3 km b) 5 km c) 8 km d) 13 km

Answer: a) 3 km North 5, then east 3, then south 5 → he is 3 km east of the start.

Q14. A person facing north turns right, then right again, then left. Which direction is he facing now? a) North b) South c) East d) West

Answer: c) East North → (right) East → (right) South → (left) East.

Q15. A walks 4 km south, then 3 km east, then 4 km north. How far is he from the start? a) 3 km b) 4 km c) 5 km d) 7 km

Answer: a) 3 km The south and north movements cancel; only 3 km east remains.

Q16. At sunset, a man's shadow falls towards: a) North b) South c) East d) West

Answer: c) East At sunset the sun is in the west, so the shadow falls east.

E. Seating & ranking (Q17–Q20)

Q17. Five friends A, B, C, D and E sit in a row facing north. A is at the extreme left and D at the extreme right. B is to the right of A but to the left of C. E sits between C and D. Who is in the middle? a) B b) C c) D d) E

Answer: b) C The order is A – B – C – E – D. The third position (middle) is C.

Q18. In a row of 20 students, Rahim is 7th from the left. What is his position from the right? a) 12th b) 13th c) 14th d) 15th

Answer: c) 14th Position from right = total − position from left + 1 = 20 − 7 + 1 = 14.

Q19. A is taller than B but shorter than C. D is taller than C. Who is the tallest? a) A b) B c) C d) D

Answer: d) D D > C > A > B.

Q20. Among five friends, P is shorter than Q but taller than R. S is taller than Q. T is shorter than R. Who is the shortest? a) P b) R c) T d) Q

Answer: c) T S > Q > P > R > T.

F. Grouping & sets (Q21–Q24)

Q21. A team of 3 is to be selected from A, B, C, D and E. A must be included, and B and C cannot be together. How many such teams are possible? a) 4 b) 5 c) 6 d) 7

Answer: b) 5 With A fixed, choose 2 from {B, C, D, E} = 6 combinations. Remove the one with B and C together → 5.

Q22. In a class, 30 students play cricket, 20 play football and 10 play both. How many play at least one game? a) 30 b) 40 c) 50 d) 60

Answer: b) 40 30 + 20 − 10 = 40.

Q23. If no cats are dogs and some dogs are pets, which conclusion is definitely true? a) No pets are cats b) Some pets are dogs c) All dogs are pets d) Some cats are pets

Answer: b) Some pets are dogs This is given directly by "some dogs are pets".

Q24. In a group of 60 people, 35 read English newspapers and 30 read Bangla newspapers. If everyone reads at least one, how many read both? a) 5 b) 10 c) 15 d) 25

Answer: a) 5 35 + 30 − 60 = 5.

G. Syllogism (Q25–Q27)

Q25. All pens are books. All books are tables. Therefore: a) All tables are pens b) All pens are tables c) Some tables are not books d) No pen is a table

Answer: b) All pens are tables

Q26. Some doctors are teachers. All teachers are honest. Therefore: a) All doctors are honest b) Some doctors are honest c) No teacher is a doctor d) All honest people are teachers

Answer: b) Some doctors are honest The doctors who are teachers are honest.

Q27. No cats are dogs. Some dogs are brown. Therefore: a) Some brown things are not cats b) All brown things are dogs c) No brown thing is a cat d) Some cats are brown

Answer: a) Some brown things are not cats The brown dogs are not cats.

H. Clock & calendar (Q28–Q30)

Q28. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 3:00? a) 30° b) 60° c) 90° d) 120°

Answer: c) 90° Each hour = 30°. 3 × 30 = 90°.

Q29. If today is Monday, what day will it be after 61 days? a) Friday b) Saturday c) Sunday d) Monday

Answer: b) Saturday 61 ÷ 7 leaves remainder 5. Monday + 5 days = Saturday.

Q30. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 3:15? a) 0° b) 7.5° c) 15° d) 30°

Answer: b) 7.5° Minute hand = 90°. Hour hand = 3 × 30 + 15 × 0.5 = 97.5°. Difference = 7.5°.
