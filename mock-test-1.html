Mock Test 1 — Full Paper (100 Questions)

On this page

  • Section C — Analytical (Q56–Q70)

Time: 90 minutes · Marks: 100 · No calculator

Instructions: Do not turn to the answer key until you have finished. Mark every answer on a separate sheet. If a question takes more than 60 seconds, mark it and move on.

Section A — English (Q1–Q25)

1. He ___ to school every day. a) go b) goes c) going d) gone

2. They ___ football now. a) play b) plays c) are playing d) played

3. I ___ my homework before he came. a) finish b) finished c) had finished d) have finished

4. She has lived here ___ 2010. a) for b) since c) from d) in

5. The news ___ shocking. a) were b) was c) are d) have

6. One of my friends ___ a doctor. a) are b) were c) is d) have

7. He is good ___ English. a) in b) at c) on d) for

8. Neither he nor I ___ present. a) is b) are c) am d) was

9. Choose the correctly spelled word. a) recieve b) receive c) receve d) recive

10. Choose the correctly spelled word. a) seperate b) separate c) seperete d) seprate

11. Synonym of diligent: a) lazy b) hardworking c) careless d) slow

12. Antonym of scarce: a) rare b) limited c) abundant d) short

13. "At stake" means: a) at risk b) at once c) at last d) at best

14. Passive form of "She wrote a letter." a) A letter is written by her. b) A letter was written by her. c) A letter has written by her. d) A letter were written by her.

15. He said, "I will come tomorrow." → Indirect: a) He said he will come tomorrow. b) He said he would come the next day. c) He says he would come tomorrow. d) He said he would come tomorrow.

16. If it ___ tomorrow, we will cancel the match. a) rain b) rains c) rained d) will rain

17. He is used to ___ in cold weather. a) work b) working c) worked d) works

18. I look forward to ___ you. a) see b) seeing c) saw d) seen

19. The man ___ is standing there is my uncle. a) which b) whom c) who d) whose

20. She sings ___ than her sister. a) good b) well c) better d) best

21. Would you mind ___ a moment? a) wait b) waiting c) to wait d) waited

22. Choose the correct sentence. a) He discussed about the matter. b) He discussed the matter. c) He discussed on the matter. d) He discussed for the matter.

23. We congratulated him ___ his promotion. a) for b) on c) at d) about

24. He is ___ M.A. in Economics. a) a b) an c) the d) no article

25. Book : Read :: Food : ? a) Cook b) Eat c) Buy d) Sell

Section B — Mathematics (Q26–Q55)

26. 15% of 400 = ? a) 50 b) 55 c) 60 d) 65

27. 250 increased by 20% = ? a) 270 b) 290 c) 300 d) 320

28. CP = Tk 250, SP = Tk 300. Profit % = ? a) 15% b) 20% c) 25% d) 30%

29. A 25% discount on Tk 800 gives a selling price of: a) Tk 600 b) Tk 620 c) Tk 640 d) Tk 700

30. Successive discounts of 20% and 10% equal a single discount of: a) 26% b) 28% c) 30% d) 32%

31. Tk 144 is divided in the ratio 5 : 7. The smaller share is: a) Tk 60 b) Tk 64 c) Tk 72 d) Tk 84

32. The average of 5 numbers is 18. Their sum is: a) 80 b) 85 c) 90 d) 95

33. The average of the first 10 natural numbers is: a) 5 b) 5.5 c) 6 d) 6.5

34. A father is 3 times as old as his son. Their total age is 48. The son's age is: a) 10 b) 12 c) 14 d) 16

35. A can do a work in 10 days and B in 15 days. Together they take: a) 5 days b) 6 days c) 7 days d) 8 days

36. 12 men can do a job in 8 days. 6 men will take: a) 12 days b) 14 days c) 16 days d) 18 days

37. A pipe fills a tank in 4 hours; a drain empties it in 6 hours. Both open — the tank fills in: a) 8 hours b) 10 hours c) 12 hours d) 14 hours

38. A car covers 90 km in 2 hours. Its speed is: a) 40 km/h b) 45 km/h c) 50 km/h d) 55 km/h

39. A 120 m train at 36 km/h crosses a pole in: a) 10 s b) 12 s c) 14 s d) 16 s

40. A boat goes 15 km/h downstream and 9 km/h upstream. Its speed in still water is: a) 10 km/h b) 11 km/h c) 12 km/h d) 13 km/h

41. Simple interest on Tk 2,000 at 5% for 2 years: a) Tk 150 b) Tk 200 c) Tk 250 d) Tk 300

42. Compound interest on Tk 1,000 at 10% for 2 years: a) Tk 200 b) Tk 210 c) Tk 220 d) Tk 240

43. The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 7. Reversing the digits increases the number by 27. The number is: a) 25 b) 34 c) 43 d) 52

44. Two-thirds of a number is 48. The number is: a) 64 b) 68 c) 72 d) 76

45. If 3x − 5 = 16, then x = ? a) 5 b) 6 c) 7 d) 8

46. The LCM of 6, 8 and 12 is: a) 12 b) 24 c) 36 d) 48

47. The largest two-digit number divisible by 7 is: a) 91 b) 94 c) 98 d) 99

48. 40% of the students in a school are girls. If there are 300 boys, the total number of students is: a) 450 b) 480 c) 500 d) 550

49. 40 litres of a mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 3 : 1. The quantity of milk is: a) 10 L b) 20 L c) 30 L d) 35 L

50. In what ratio must tea at Tk 20/kg be mixed with tea at Tk 30/kg to get a mixture worth Tk 24/kg? a) 2 : 3 b) 3 : 2 c) 1 : 2 d) 3 : 5

51. A rectangle is 15 m by 10 m. Its area and perimeter are: a) 150 m², 50 m b) 150 m², 25 m c) 50 m², 150 m d) 160 m², 50 m

52. The area of a circle of diameter 14 cm is: a) 144 cm² b) 154 cm² c) 164 cm² d) 176 cm²

53. The area of a square of side 6 cm is: a) 24 cm² b) 30 cm² c) 36 cm² d) 42 cm²

54. Next in the series 5, 10, 20, 40, ___: a) 60 b) 70 c) 80 d) 90

55. The probability of getting an even number when a die is thrown: a) 1/6 b) 1/3 c) 1/2 d) 2/3

Section C — Analytical (Q56–Q70)

56. Next in the series 1, 4, 9, 16, ___: a) 20 b) 24 c) 25 d) 36

57. Next in the series 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ___: a) 18 b) 20 c) 21 d) 23

58. If SUN is coded as TVO, how is MOON coded? a) NPPO b) NOPP c) NPPN d) OPPO

59. A is the son of B. B is the sister of C. How is C related to A? a) Father b) Uncle c) Brother d) Grandfather

60. A man walks 3 km north and then 4 km east. How far is he from the start? a) 3 km b) 4 km c) 5 km d) 7 km

61. In a row of 40 students, Karim is 15th from the left. His position from the right is: a) 25th b) 26th c) 27th d) 28th

62. Five people sit in a row facing north. P is at the extreme left, S at the extreme right. Q is between P and R. Who is in the middle? a) P b) Q c) R d) S

63. All men are mortal. Socrates is a man. Therefore: a) Socrates is immortal b) Socrates is mortal c) All mortals are men d) Socrates is not a man

64. Odd one out: 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 a) 3 b) 5 c) 9 d) 11

65. Odd one out: apple, banana, carrot, mango a) apple b) banana c) carrot d) mango

66. The angle between the hands of a clock at 6:00 is: a) 90° b) 120° c) 150° d) 180°

67. If today is Monday, what day will it be after 30 days? a) Tuesday b) Wednesday c) Thursday d) Friday

68. A is twice as old as B. Their total age is 36. A's age is: a) 12 b) 18 c) 24 d) 28

69. 2 : 8 :: 3 : ? a) 9 b) 12 c) 18 d) 27

70. Next in the series A, C, E, G, ___: a) H b) I c) J d) K
