Mock Test 2 — Short Paper (50 Questions)

Sit Mock Test 2 properly

50 questions, 45 minutes, auto-marked with a section breakdown.

Time: 45 minutes · Marks: 50 · No calculator

A shorter, slightly harder paper for the second week. Do it under exam conditions: phone away, one sitting, no pauses.

Section A — English (Q1–Q12)

1. She ___ here since morning. a) waits b) is waiting c) has been waiting d) waited

2. Each of the boys ___ a prize. a) get b) gets c) are getting d) have got

3. He is junior ___ me. a) than b) to c) of d) from

4. I am looking forward to ___ from you. a) hear b) hearing c) heard d) hears

5. Choose the correctly spelled word. a) privilege b) priviledge c) privilage d) privileg

6. Synonym of prudent: a) reckless b) wise c) rich d) hasty

7. Antonym of candid: a) frank b) honest c) evasive d) open

8. "In the wake of" means: a) before b) following c) despite d) instead of

9. Passive form of "They have completed the work." a) The work has completed. b) The work has been completed. c) The work is completed by them. d) The work was completed.

10. He said, "Where do you live?" → Indirect speech: a) He asked where I lived. b) He asked where do I live. c) He asked where did I live. d) He said where I lived.

11. If I ___ rich, I would travel the world. a) am b) was c) were d) be

12. Choose the correct sentence. a) One of my friends are a doctor. b) One of my friends is a doctor. c) One of my friend is a doctor. d) One of my friends were a doctor.

Section B — Mathematics (Q13–Q27)

13. 12.5% of 800 = ? a) 90 b) 100 c) 110 d) 120

14. A shirt is marked at Tk 800. A 10% discount is given, then 5% VAT is added on the discounted price. The final price is: a) Tk 750 b) Tk 756 c) Tk 760 d) Tk 770

15. A trader gains 20% by selling an item for Tk 600. The cost price is: a) Tk 480 b) Tk 500 c) Tk 520 d) Tk 540

16. Tk 64 is divided in the ratio 3 : 5. The larger share is: a) Tk 32 b) Tk 36 c) Tk 40 d) Tk 44

17. The average of 8 numbers is 20. If the number 27 is removed, the average of the remaining numbers is: a) 18 b) 19 c) 19.5 d) 20

18. A can do a work in 8 days and B in 12 days. Working together they take: a) 4 days b) 4.8 days c) 5 days d) 5.5 days

19. 15 men can do a work in 20 days. How many men are needed to do it in 12 days? a) 20 b) 22 c) 25 d) 30

20. A train 180 m long runs at 54 km/h. How long does it take to cross a platform 270 m long? a) 24 s b) 27 s c) 30 s d) 33 s

21. A boat's speed in still water is 10 km/h and the current is 2 km/h. How long does it take to go 24 km downstream? a) 1.5 hours b) 2 hours c) 2.5 hours d) 3 hours

22. Simple interest on Tk 4,500 at 4% per annum for 5 years is: a) Tk 800 b) Tk 900 c) Tk 1,000 d) Tk 1,100

23. Compound interest on Tk 2,000 at 5% per annum for 2 years is: a) Tk 200 b) Tk 205 c) Tk 210 d) Tk 220

24. The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 11. Reversing the digits increases the number by 45. The number is: a) 29 b) 38 c) 47 d) 56

25. 80 litres of a mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 5 : 3. How much water must be added to make the ratio 5 : 4? a) 8 L b) 10 L c) 12 L d) 15 L

26. The perimeter of a rectangle is 40 m and its length is 12 m. Its area is: a) 96 m² b) 100 m² c) 108 m² d) 120 m²

27. Next in the series 4, 9, 16, 25, ___: a) 32 b) 36 c) 40 d) 49

Section C — Analytical (Q28–Q35)

28. If A = 1, B = 2, C = 3 …, the value of the word BAD is: a) 6 b) 7 c) 8 d) 9

29. Pointing to a woman, Rahim said, "She is the daughter of my mother's only son." How is she related to Rahim? a) Sister b) Daughter c) Niece d) Mother

30. A man facing south turns left, then left again. Which direction is he facing now? a) East b) North c) South d) West

31. In a row of 25 students, Jamal is 9th from the right. What is his position from the left? a) 16th b) 17th c) 18th d) 19th

32. All roses are flowers. Some flowers fade quickly. Which conclusion follows? a) All roses fade quickly b) Some roses fade quickly c) No rose fades quickly d) None of these follows

33. Odd one out: 2, 4, 6, 9, 10 a) 2 b) 4 c) 9 d) 10

34. The angle between the hands of a clock at 2:00 is: a) 30° b) 45° c) 60° d) 90°

35. If today is Friday, what day will it be after 45 days? a) Sunday b) Monday c) Tuesday d) Wednesday

Section D — General Knowledge (Q36–Q45)

36. Bangladesh Bank was established under the Bangladesh Bank Order of: a) 1971 b) 1972 c) 1991 d) 1994

37. The Standing Lending Facility (SLF) rate is: a) 7.50% b) 9.50% c) 11.00% d) 12.00%

38. The Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate is: a) 7.50% b) 8.50% c) 9.50% d) 11.00%

39. The 12-month average inflation in Bangladesh in September 2026 was: a) 7.50% b) 8.65% c) 9.16% d) 10.03%

40. The GDP growth target in the FY2026-27 budget is: a) 5.50% b) 6.00% c) 6.50% d) 7.50%

41. The Managing Director & CEO of Dutch-Bangla Bank PLC is: a) Md. Ahteshamul Haque Khan b) Md. Mostaqur Rahman c) Ahsan H. Mansur d) Abdur Rouf Talukder

42. BEFTN is best described as: a) A real-time gross settlement system b) A batch-based electronic funds transfer network c) A card switch d) A cheque clearing house

43. NPSB stands for: a) National Payment Switch Bangladesh b) National Public Service Bank c) New Payment Security Board d) National Payroll Settlement Bureau

44. The national fish of Bangladesh is: a) Rui b) Katla c) Hilsa d) Pangash

45. The Asian Development Bank is headquartered in: a) Tokyo b) Beijing c) Manila d) Singapore

Section E — ICT (Q46–Q50)

46. Ctrl + F is used to: a) Find b) Format c) Freeze d) Fill

47. 1 GB equals: a) 1000 MB b) 1024 MB c) 1024 KB d) 512 MB

48. Which of the following is volatile memory? a) ROM b) RAM c) Hard disk d) DVD

49. In MS Excel, which formula adds the values in cells A1 to A5? a) =ADD(A1:A5) b) =SUM(A1:A5) c) =TOTAL(A1:A5) d) =PLUS(A1:A5)

50. The first generation of computers used: a) Transistors b) Vacuum tubes c) Integrated circuits d) Microprocessors
