## Notes on the questions that matter

### Section A — English

**Q1 (b) goes** — "Every day" signals simple present; third person singular takes -s.

**Q3 (c) had finished** — Two past actions; the earlier one takes past perfect. "Before he came" is the later event.

**Q5 (b) was** — "News" ends in -s but is singular and uncountable.

**Q6 (c) is** — "One of + plural noun" → the subject is "one", so singular verb.

**Q8 (c) am** — Neither…nor takes the verb of the **nearer** subject. Nearer subject is "I" → am. This is the single most common trap in the paper.

**Q9 (b) receive** — i before e except after c.

**Q12 (c) abundant** — "Scarce" means rare/insufficient; the antonym is plentiful/abundant. (a) and (b) are synonyms, not antonyms — read the question word carefully.

**Q15 (b)** — Reported speech shifts "will" → "would" and "tomorrow" → "the next day". Option (d) shifts only the verb, which is half-right; the time word must shift too.

**Q16 (b) rains** — First conditional: if + present simple, will + base verb.

**Q17 (b) working** — "Be used to" + gerund. Compare "used to work" (past habit) — different structure.

**Q20 (c) better** — Comparative of "well" is "better"; "than" confirms the comparative.

**Q22 (b)** — "Discuss" is transitive; no "about".

**Q24 (b) an** — "M.A." is pronounced "em-ay", beginning with a vowel sound.

### Section B — Mathematics

**Q26 (c) 60** — 0.15 × 400.

**Q27 (c) 300** — 250 × 1.20.

**Q28 (b) 20%** — Profit = 50; 50/250 × 100 = 20%. Profit % is always on **cost** price.

**Q30 (b) 28%** — 100 → 80 → 72. Or 20 + 10 − 2 = 28.

**Q31 (a) Tk 60** — 12 parts; 1 part = 12; smaller share = 5 × 12.

**Q33 (b) 5.5** — (10 + 1)/2.

**Q34 (b) 12** — 3x + x = 48 → x = 12 (son), father 36.

**Q35 (b) 6 days** — (10 × 15)/25 = 6.

**Q36 (c) 16 days** — 12 × 8 = 96 man-days; 96/6 = 16.

**Q37 (c) 12 hours** — 1/4 − 1/6 = 1/12.

**Q39 (b) 12 s** — 36 km/h = 10 m/s; 120/10.

**Q40 (c) 12 km/h** — (15 + 9)/2.

**Q42 (b) Tk 210** — 1000 × 1.21 − 1000.

**Q43 (a) 25** — Let the number be 10a + b, a + b = 7, and 9(b − a) = 27 → b − a = 3 → a = 2, b = 5.

**Q47 (c) 98** — 98 = 7 × 14; 99 is not divisible by 7.

**Q48 (c) 500** — Boys are 60% → 300/0.60 = 500.

**Q50 (b) 3 : 2** — Alligation: (30 − 24) : (24 − 20) = 6 : 4.

**Q52 (b) 154 cm²** — Diameter 14 → radius 7 → (22/7) × 49.

**Q55 (c) 1/2** — Even faces 2, 4, 6 → 3/6.

### Section C — Analytical

**Q58 (a) NPPO** — Each letter +1: M→N, O→P, O→P, N→O.

**Q59 (b) Uncle** — B is A's mother; C is B's brother → maternal uncle.

**Q61 (b) 26th** — 40 − 15 + 1 = 26.

**Q62 (b) Q — but the question is flawed.**
The stated clues are: P at the extreme left, S at the extreme right, and "Q is between P and R". With five seats, the clues allow several arrangements (P–Q–R–S–X, P–R–Q–S–X, etc.), so the middle seat is not uniquely determined. The **intended** arrangement is P–Q–R–S–(fifth), making Q third.
**Lesson:** in the real hall, if a seating clue is genuinely ambiguous, answer the arrangement that satisfies *all* clues most naturally, and do not spend more than 60 seconds on it. One mark is not worth three.

**Q64 (c) 9** — The only composite number in the list.

**Q65 (c) carrot** — The only vegetable; the rest are fruits.

**Q67 (b) Wednesday** — 30 ÷ 7 leaves remainder 2 → Monday + 2.

**Q69 (d) 27** — 8 = 2³, so the answer is 3³.

**Q75 (b) 9.50%** — Repo, effective 2 August 2026.

**Q76 (b) 8.34%** — Point-to-point, September 2026 (12-month average 8.65%).

**Q77 (b) 4.14%** — FY2025-26 provisional.

**Q81 (c) 193** — UN member states.

**Q88 (b) Bangladesh Bank** — MFS licensing and regulation sit with BB (BTRC regulates telecom, not payments).
