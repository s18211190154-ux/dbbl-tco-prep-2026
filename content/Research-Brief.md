## 2. What previous DBBL papers actually look like

Two solved DBBL papers were located and read page by page (scans saved in `TCO-Prep-2026/00-Past-Papers-Scanned/`).

### DBBL Probationary Officer — held 15 November 2019
- **Total marks 100; time 90 minutes**
- **Part I — MCQ (50 marks):**
  - English: vocabulary fill-in-the-blank, odd-word-out, spelling, sentence correction
  - Mathematics: number concept, age, percentage, geometry, algebra, ratio
  - Analytical: logic games (shelf/grouping puzzles), IQ (mirror-image clock), series
  - General Knowledge: Bangladesh + international + ICT (MS PowerPoint, MS Word)
- **Part II — Descriptive (50 marks):**
  - Problem solving (2 questions, show calculations) — Venn diagram set problem, tree-planting geometry
  - Critical writing (agree/disagree essay)
  - Essay writing (15 marks) — "Innovation and National Development"

### DBBL Probationary Officer — held 18 August 2017
- **Total marks 90; time 60 minutes**
- Same subject mix: English grammar/vocabulary, math (time & work, number concept, inequality, profit & loss, percentage), analytical reasoning, GK, ICT
- Written part: profit & loss, mixture, set theory, speed/distance, plus descriptive writing

### Observed topic frequency (from the two papers + published question banks)
- **Math:** profit & loss, percentage, ratio, average, time & work, speed/distance, simple & compound interest, number concept, age, mixture, sets/Venn, geometry, series
- **English:** sentence correction, fill-in-the-blank (vocabulary and grammar), spelling, synonym/antonym, analogy, idioms, odd word out
- **Analytical:** linear/circular seating, grouping & selection, blood relations, coding-decoding, direction sense, series, syllogism, data sufficiency
- **GK:** Bangladesh districts/ports/forests, constitution amendments, national awards, sports, UN bodies, SDG/ADB, and **banking/economy**
- **ICT:** MS Word/PowerPoint shortcuts, computer generations, networking, internet security

### One coaching analysis of DBBL's last four circulars
A Bangladeshi news/coaching article states DBBL's preliminary MCQ is **50 marks** (English 20, Math 10, Analytical 10, GK+ICT 10) and the written paper is **40 marks** (focused writing, argument writing, written math). This matches the 2019 paper's Part I/Part II split closely. It is secondary analysis, not official. Note that the article is written around the MTO/Officer level.

**Inference for TCO:** TCO is a junior post, so the paper is likely *similar in structure but easier in difficulty*, and may include a Bangla section (common in junior private-bank posts). A candidate should prepare for the full set: Bangla, English, Math, Analytical, GK/Banking, ICT, plus short descriptive writing.
