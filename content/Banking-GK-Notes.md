## 2. Structure of the banking system

- **Scheduled banks** — licensed commercial banks under the Bank Company Act 1991.
- **Categories:**
  1. State-Owned Commercial Banks (SOCB) — Sonali, Janata, Agrani, Rupali, BASIC, BDBL
  2. Specialised banks — BKB, RAKUB, BSRS, Probashi Kallyan Bank
  3. Private Commercial Banks (PCB) — conventional and Islamic
  4. Foreign Commercial Banks (FCB)
- **Islamic banking** operates under Shariah; SLR 5.5%.
- **Non-bank financial institutions (NBFI)** — leasing, finance companies.

### Regulators and bodies

| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| Bangladesh Bank | Central bank, monetary policy, bank supervision |
| BFIU | Bangladesh Financial Intelligence Unit — AML/CFT (established 2012) |
| BSEC | Securities regulator |
| IDRA | Insurance regulator |
| Microcredit Regulatory Authority | MFI regulator |

## 3. The glossary they test

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Repo** | Rate at which BB lends to banks against securities (short-term) |
| **Reverse repo** | Rate at which BB borrows from banks |
| **CRR** | Cash Reserve Ratio — cash kept with BB, earns no interest |
| **SLR** | Statutory Liquidity Ratio — liquid assets (cash, gold, govt securities) |
| **Call money** | Overnight interbank lending rate |
| **CAMELS** | Capital adequacy, Asset quality, Management, Earnings, Liquidity, Sensitivity to market risk — bank rating framework |
| **NPL** | Non-Performing Loan — loan on which interest/principal is overdue |
| **CRAR / CAR** | Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets Ratio — minimum 10% (12.5% with buffer under Basel III) |
| **Basel III** | International capital and liquidity standards |
| **ADR** | Advance–Deposit Ratio |
| **LCR** | Liquidity Coverage Ratio — ≥100% (30-day stress) |
| **NSFR** | Net Stable Funding Ratio — >100% (1-year funding stability) |
| **KYC** | Know Your Customer |
| **AML / CFT** | Anti-Money Laundering / Combating Financing of Terrorism |
| **Nostro / Vostro** | Our account with them / their account with us |
| **Letter of Credit (LC)** | Bank's payment guarantee in trade |
| **Bank guarantee** | Bank's promise to pay if the customer defaults |
| **Mortgage** | Loan secured by immovable property |
| **Hypothecation** | Charge on movable assets without transfer of possession |
| **Pledge** | Charge with transfer of possession |
| **Lien** | Right to retain goods/property until a debt is paid |
| **Repo tenor** | Usually 1–7 days in Bangladesh |

## 4. Digital payments and channels

| System | What it is |
|---|---|
| **RTGS** | Real Time Gross Settlement — high-value, real-time interbank transfers |
| **BEFTN** | Bangladesh Electronic Funds Transfer Network — batch, low-value transfers |
| **NPSB** | National Payment Switch Bangladesh — interbank ATM/POS/QR switching |
| **MFS** | Mobile Financial Services — bKash, Nagad, **Rocket (DBBL)**, Upay, SureCash, mCash |
| **Agent banking** | Banking through appointed agents, mainly rural |
| **ATM / CRM** | Cash withdrawal / cash recycling machine (deposit + withdrawal) |
| **POS** | Point of Sale terminal |
| **EMV chip** | Chip-based card security standard |
| **Bangla QR** | Interoperable QR payment standard |
| **Nexus** | DBBL's card brand / network |
| **NexusPay** | DBBL's mobile wallet app |

**Payment flow to remember:** customer → channel (branch/ATM/agent/MFS) → switch (NPSB) → clearing (BEFTN/RTGS) → settlement at Bangladesh Bank.

## 8. Five definitions to be able to write in one sentence

1. **Bank:** a financial institution that accepts deposits, lends money and provides payment services.
2. **Central bank:** the institution that manages a country's currency, money supply and banking system.
3. **Inflation:** a sustained rise in the general price level, which reduces purchasing power.
4. **Non-performing loan:** a loan on which the borrower has stopped paying interest or principal for the prescribed period.
5. **Financial inclusion:** ensuring that all people, especially low-income and rural groups, have access to affordable financial services.
