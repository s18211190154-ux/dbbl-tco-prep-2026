## Section A — English (Q1–Q12)

**1.** She ___ here since morning.
a) waits b) is waiting c) has been waiting d) waited

**2.** Each of the boys ___ a prize.
a) get b) gets c) are getting d) have got

**3.** He is junior ___ me.
a) than b) to c) of d) from

**4.** I am looking forward to ___ from you.
a) hear b) hearing c) heard d) hears

**5.** Choose the correctly spelled word.
a) privilege b) priviledge c) privilage d) privileg

**6.** Synonym of **prudent**:
a) reckless b) wise c) rich d) hasty

**7.** Antonym of **candid**:
a) frank b) honest c) evasive d) open

**8.** "In the wake of" means:
a) before b) following c) despite d) instead of

**9.** Passive form of "They have completed the work."
a) The work has completed. b) The work has been completed. c) The work is completed by them. d) The work was completed.

**10.** He said, "Where do you live?" → Indirect speech:
a) He asked where I lived. b) He asked where do I live. c) He asked where did I live. d) He said where I lived.

**11.** If I ___ rich, I would travel the world.
a) am b) was c) were d) be

**12.** Choose the correct sentence.
a) One of my friends are a doctor. b) One of my friends is a doctor. c) One of my friend is a doctor. d) One of my friends were a doctor.
