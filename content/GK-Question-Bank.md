## A. Banking & Economy (Q1–Q22)

**Q1.** Bangladesh Bank was established on:
a) 26 March 1971 b) 16 December 1971 c) 4 November 1972 d) 16 December 1972

**Answer: b) 16 December 1971**

**Q2.** Who is the current Governor of Bangladesh Bank?
a) Ahsan H. Mansur b) Md. Mostaqur Rahman c) Abdur Rouf Talukder d) Fazle Kabir

**Answer: b) Md. Mostaqur Rahman** (took office February 2026)

**Q3.** What is the current policy (repo) rate of Bangladesh Bank?
a) 8.50% b) 9.00% c) 9.50% d) 10.00%

**Answer: c) 9.50%** (effective 2 August 2026)

**Q4.** The Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) for conventional scheduled banks is:
a) 4.00% b) 5.50% c) 13.00% d) 18.00%

**Answer: c) 13.00%**

**Q5.** The Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) for scheduled banks in Bangladesh is:
a) 3.00% b) 4.00% c) 5.50% d) 13.00%

**Answer: b) 4.00%** (bi-weekly average; daily requirement 3.00%)

**Q6.** The Standing Lending Facility (SLF) rate is:
a) 7.50% b) 9.50% c) 11.00% d) 11.50%

**Answer: c) 11.00%**

**Q7.** The Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate is:
a) 7.50% b) 8.00% c) 9.50% d) 11.00%

**Answer: a) 7.50%**

**Q8.** Headline inflation in Bangladesh in September 2026 was approximately:
a) 6.34% b) 7.50% c) 8.34% d) 9.42%

**Answer: c) 8.34%** (point-to-point; 12-month average 8.65%)

**Q9.** Gross foreign exchange reserves of Bangladesh at the end of September 2026 were approximately:
a) USD 26 billion b) USD 31 billion c) USD 36 billion d) USD 45 billion

**Answer: c) USD 36 billion**

**Q10.** Real GDP growth of Bangladesh in FY2025-26 (provisional) was:
a) 3.49% b) 4.14% c) 5.20% d) 6.50%

**Answer: b) 4.14%**

**Q11.** The GDP growth target in the FY2026-27 budget is:
a) 5.50% b) 6.00% c) 6.50% d) 7.50%

**Answer: c) 6.50%**

**Q12.** In the CAMELS rating framework, the letter "L" stands for:
a) Leverage b) Liquidity c) Liability d) Loan

**Answer: b) Liquidity**

**Q13.** NPL in banking stands for:
a) New Private Loan b) Non-Performing Loan c) National Priority Lending d) Net Public Liability

**Answer: b) Non-Performing Loan**

**Q14.** RTGS is used mainly for:
a) Small retail transfers b) High-value real-time interbank transfers c) Mobile top-ups d) Foreign currency trading

**Answer: b) High-value real-time interbank transfers**

**Q15.** BEFTN is best described as:
a) A real-time gross settlement system b) A batch-based electronic funds transfer network c) A card switch d) A cheque clearing house

**Answer: b) A batch-based electronic funds transfer network**

**Q16.** NPSB stands for:
a) National Payment Switch Bangladesh b) National Public Service Bank c) New Payment Security Board d) National Payroll Settlement Bureau

**Answer: a) National Payment Switch Bangladesh**

**Q17.** Dutch-Bangla Bank commenced banking operations in:
a) 1994 b) 1995 c) 1996 d) 1998

**Answer: c) 1996** (3 June 1996)

**Q18.** "Rocket" is the mobile financial service of:
a) bKash b) Nagad c) Dutch-Bangla Bank d) BRAC Bank

**Answer: c) Dutch-Bangla Bank**

**Q19.** BFIU is mainly responsible for:
a) Bank licensing b) Anti-money laundering and combating financing of terrorism c) Deposit insurance d) Stock market regulation

**Answer: b) Anti-money laundering and combating financing of terrorism**

**Q20.** The minimum Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets Ratio (CRAR) for banks in Bangladesh is:
a) 8% b) 10% c) 12.5% d) 15%

**Answer: b) 10%** (12.5% including the capital conservation buffer)

**Q21.** Basel III is a set of international standards on:
a) Accounting b) Bank capital and liquidity c) Trade tariffs d) Insurance claims

**Answer: b) Bank capital and liquidity**

**Q22.** The Money Laundering Prevention Act of Bangladesh was enacted in:
a) 2009 b) 2010 c) 2012 d) 2015

**Answer: c) 2012**

## B. Bangladesh (Q23–Q42)

**Q23.** Independence Day of Bangladesh is observed on:
a) 16 December b) 26 March c) 21 February d) 7 March

**Answer: b) 26 March**

**Q24.** Victory Day of Bangladesh is:
a) 26 March b) 14 December c) 16 December d) 21 February

**Answer: c) 16 December**

**Q25.** The Constitution of Bangladesh came into effect on:
a) 4 November 1972 b) 16 December 1972 c) 26 March 1972 d) 1 January 1973

**Answer: b) 16 December 1972**

**Q26.** The Padma Bridge was opened to traffic on:
a) 25 June 2022 b) 26 March 2022 c) 16 December 2021 d) 1 July 2022

**Answer: a) 25 June 2022**

**Q27.** The length of the Padma Bridge is approximately:
a) 4.8 km b) 6.15 km c) 9.8 km d) 12 km

**Answer: b) 6.15 km**

**Q28.** The first phase of Dhaka Metro Rail was opened in:
a) December 2021 b) December 2022 c) June 2023 d) December 2023

**Answer: b) December 2022** (28 December 2022)

**Q29.** Rooppur Nuclear Power Plant is located in which district?
a) Pabna b) Natore c) Kushtia d) Rajshahi

**Answer: a) Pabna**

**Q30.** The Sundarbans was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in:
a) 1985 b) 1992 c) 1997 d) 2001

**Answer: c) 1997**

**Q31.** The only coral island of Bangladesh is:
a) Sandwip b) St. Martin's c) Kutubdia d) Hatiya

**Answer: b) St. Martin's**

**Q32.** The largest district of Bangladesh by area is:
a) Chattogram b) Rangamati c) Khulna d) Mymensingh

**Answer: b) Rangamati**

**Q33.** The smallest district of Bangladesh by area is:
a) Narayanganj b) Meherpur c) Dhaka d) Chapainawabganj

**Answer: a) Narayanganj**

**Q34.** The third seaport of Bangladesh is:
a) Chattogram b) Mongla c) Payra d) Sonadia

**Answer: c) Payra** (Patuakhali)

**Q35.** The national bird of Bangladesh is:
a) Crow b) Doyel (magpie robin) c) Parrot d) Kingfisher

**Answer: b) Doyel (magpie robin)**

**Q36.** The national fish of Bangladesh is:
a) Rui b) Katla c) Hilsa d) Pangash

**Answer: c) Hilsa**

**Q37.** The national flower of Bangladesh is:
a) Rose b) Water lily (shapla) c) Marigold d) Lotus

**Answer: b) Water lily (shapla)**

**Q38.** How many divisions are there in Bangladesh?
a) 6 b) 7 c) 8 d) 9

**Answer: c) 8** (Mymensingh Division was created in 2015)

**Q39.** According to the 2022 census, the population of Bangladesh is approximately:
a) 14.5 crore b) 16.98 crore c) 18.2 crore d) 20 crore

**Answer: b) 16.98 crore**

**Q40.** Bangabandhu Satellite-1 was launched in:
a) 2016 b) 2017 c) 2018 d) 2019

**Answer: c) 2018** (11 May 2018)

**Q41.** Burimari land port is situated in which district?
a) Kurigram b) Rangpur c) Lalmonirhat d) Nilphamari

**Answer: c) Lalmonirhat**

**Q42.** Ratargul Swamp Forest is located in:
a) Sylhet b) Moulvibazar c) Sunamganj d) Habiganj

**Answer: a) Sylhet**

## D. Economy Basics (Q53–Q60)

**Q53.** The largest export-earning sector of Bangladesh is:
a) Leather b) Ready-made garments c) Jute d) Frozen fish

**Answer: b) Ready-made garments**

**Q54.** The fiscal year of Bangladesh runs from:
a) January to December b) April to March c) July to June d) October to September

**Answer: c) July to June**

**Q55.** The currency symbol of Bangladesh is:
a) ₹ b) ৳ c) Rs d) Tk$

**Answer: b) ৳**

**Q56.** Inflation means:
a) A fall in the value of money only b) A sustained rise in the general price level c) A rise in wages d) A fall in exports

**Answer: b) A sustained rise in the general price level**

**Q57.** Monetary policy in Bangladesh is conducted by:
a) The Ministry of Finance b) Bangladesh Bank c) BSEC d) The Planning Commission

**Answer: b) Bangladesh Bank**

**Q58.** The repo rate is the rate at which:
a) Banks lend to customers b) Bangladesh Bank lends to commercial banks c) Banks lend to each other overnight d) The government borrows from the public

**Answer: b) Bangladesh Bank lends to commercial banks**

**Q59.** Which of the following is a direct tax?
a) VAT b) Import duty c) Income tax d) Supplementary duty

**Answer: c) Income tax**

**Q60.** GDP stands for:
a) Gross Domestic Product b) General Development Programme c) Gross Deposit Position d) Government Debt Portfolio

**Answer: a) Gross Domestic Product**
