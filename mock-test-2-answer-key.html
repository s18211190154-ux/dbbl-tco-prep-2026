Worked notes

Q1 (c) — "Since morning" marks an action continuing from a past point → present perfect continuous.

Q2 (b) — "Each" is singular.

Q3 (b) — Junior/senior/superior/inferior + to.

Q5 (a) privilege — no d; one l in the middle, -lege at the end.

Q7 (c) evasive — Candid = frank/open; the opposite is evasive.

Q9 (b) — Present perfect passive: has been completed.

Q10 (a) — Question in reported speech: no inversion, no "do"; tense shifts back.

Q11 (c) were — Second conditional, "were" for all persons.

Q12 (b) — "One of" + plural noun + singular verb.

Q13 (b) 100 — 1/8 of 800.

Q14 (b) Tk 756 — 800 × 0.90 = 720; 720 × 1.05 = 756. Do the two operations in order — discount first, then VAT.

Q15 (b) Tk 500 — 600/1.20.

Q16 (c) Tk 40 — 8 parts; 1 part = 8; larger = 5 × 8.

Q17 (b) 19 — Sum 160; remove 27 → 133; 133/7 = 19.

Q18 (b) 4.8 days — (8 × 12)/(8 + 12) = 96/20.

Q19 (c) 25 men — 15 × 20 = 300 man-days; 300/12.

Q20 (c) 30 s — 54 km/h = 15 m/s; (180 + 270)/15.

Q21 (b) 2 hours — Downstream = 10 + 2 = 12 km/h; 24/12.

Q22 (b) Tk 900 — 4500 × 4 × 5/100.

Q23 (b) Tk 205 — 2000 × (1.05)² = 2205.

Q24 (b) 38 — a + b = 11 and 9(b − a) = 45 → b − a = 5 → a = 3, b = 8.

Q25 (b) 10 L — Milk stays 50 L; water 30 L. 50/(30 + x) = 5/4 → x = 10.

Q26 (a) 96 m² — l + b = 20 → b = 8; 12 × 8.

Q27 (b) 36 — Squares 2², 3², 4², 5², 6².

Q28 (b) 7 — 2 + 1 + 4.

Q29 (b) Daughter — "My mother's only son" is Rahim himself.

Q30 (b) North — South → left = East → left = North.

Q31 (b) 17th — 25 − 9 + 1.

Q32 (d) None follows — The flowers that fade quickly need not include any rose. "Some flowers" does not connect to "roses" safely.

Q33 (c) 9 — The only odd number.

Q34 (c) 60° — 2 × 30.

Q35 (b) Monday — 45 ÷ 7 = 6 remainder 3; Friday + 3.

Q36 (b) 1972 — Bangladesh Bank Order, 1972 (the bank itself began operating 16 December 1971).

Q37 (c) 11.00% — SLF.

Q38 (a) 7.50% — SDF.

Q39 (b) 8.65% — 12-month average, September 2026 (point-to-point was 8.34%).

Q40 (c) 6.50% — FY27 budget target.

Q41 (a) — Md. Ahteshamul Haque Khan is DBBL's MD & CEO. (b) is the Bangladesh Bank Governor — do not mix them up; that swap is a favourite trick.

Q42 (b) — BEFTN is batch-based.

Q43 (a) — National Payment Switch Bangladesh.

Q44 (c) Hilsa — National fish. (National animal: Bengal tiger; national bird: Doyel; national flower: shapla.)

Q45 (c) Manila — ADB.

Q47 (b) 1024 MB.

Q48 (b) RAM — Volatile.

Q49 (b) =SUM(A1:A5).

The five mistakes that cost the most marks across both mocks

  1. Profit/loss percentage on the wrong base. Always on cost price unless the question says "on selling price".
  2. "Neither…nor" verb agreement. Look at the nearer subject.
  3. Reporting "tomorrow" without shifting it to "the next day".
  4. Mixing the Bangladesh Bank Governor with the DBBL MD. Two different people, two different institutions.
  5. Not reading "antonym" vs "synonym". Two marks per paper, gone in two seconds.
